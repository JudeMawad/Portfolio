ODDHAUS is about everyday objects with character. A little raw. A little playful. Always with a mind of their own.

CONCRETE ROOTS. COLORFUL IDEAS.

Curves, edges and small ideas.

Skateparks, painted surfaces and independent skate publications shape how we see everyday objects. Those influences run through our concrete objects, lighter covers and unusual trays.

We explore the meeting point of familiar function and unexpected form. A curve becomes a tray, a spiral catches the eye. Small shifts give everyday things an expression of their own.
