ABOUT

BRUNSWICK, GER

I’m interested in the systems behind the things we use
and in understanding, designing, and building them across software and hardware.

As a Computer Science student, I build complete systems, from software and interfaces to infrastructure, embedded computing, and physical products. What interests me most is how the pieces of a system fit together: how software is structured, how components interact, and how different technologies can be combined into something reliable, useful, and well designed.
